## Hi, I'm Zaheir 👋

**I run the operations behind live events, and I build the tools I wish I'd had.**

For three years I worked behind the stage in Lagos: logistics, accreditation, suppliers, risk plans and volunteers. I worked on 12+ events, including The Headies, Culturati and AFW Lagos, and recruited and scheduled 1,000+ volunteers.

Those volunteers are where this page starts. Every one of them gave a night of real work, and almost none of them left with anything to show for it. So I started building systems that make that work count. I'm now studying for an MSc in Business Analytics and Technologies in Manchester, to bring data into that work.

### 🟡 LOOP: where creative potential meets professional opportunity

<a href="https://loop-crew-delta.vercel.app"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zaheirrrrr/loop-showcase/main/media/desktop-stage.jpg" alt="The LOOP main stage, with the logo glowing on the screen above an illustrated crowd" /></a>

LOOP is a pathway from volunteer to intern, placement and professional for Africa's creative industry. Every role you work is logged on your **LOOP Card**, and organisers use that record to book crew and hire. It grew out of the LOOP Contribution Model, which I first designed to run a 69-person volunteer production team.

**[Live site](https://loop-crew-delta.vercel.app)** · **[How it's built](https://github.com/zaheirrrrr/loop-showcase)** · Next.js · TypeScript · Postgres · three.js
